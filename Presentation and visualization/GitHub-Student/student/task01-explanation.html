What the EDA says

Barcelona recorded 54,928 unique road-accident incidents between 2019 and 2025. Incident volume and serious harm are evolving differently. The total number of incidents fell from 10,027 in 2019 to 7,741 in 2025, a 23% reduction. However, people seriously injured increased from 202 to 249, also a 23% change but in the opposite direction. This is why we use two small line charts rather than one combined scale final graphic. Policy makers could see the contrast without mistaking fewer incidents for safer outcomes.

The operational pattern is also clear. Recorded incidents concentrate on weekday afternoons, with Friday at 14:00 the largest day-hour group. Eixample has the greatest absolute number of accidents, while Horta-Guinardo has the highest observed share of serious or fatal incidents. These findings can guide further investigation and deployment but they are not necesarilly measures of individual risk.

Data quality is generally strong. Missing district information affects 0.42% of incidents and missing coordinates 0.08%. Nevertheless, 26 duplicate incident IDs reflect multiple pedestrian/cause labels and must not inflate totals. The cleaning notes also state that blank cells in 2024 and 2025 were converted to zero, so some zeroes may mean unknown possibly introducing bias. Finally, a 53-victim event is a genuine validation priority, not a reason for automatic deletion. All figures are observed administrative records, not forecasts.
